About Zeebroo

One platform for how your business really runs

Zeebroo was built for shop owners, managers, and growing teams who are tired of juggling separate tools for the till, stock, bills, payroll, and marketing. We bring those workflows together so your numbers stay aligned from the counter to your reports.

Our mission

Give every business a calm, dependable workspace — not another pile of subscriptions — so owners can focus on customers and growth.

Who we serve

Retail shops, small chains, and operations-heavy teams that need stock, POS, accounts, HR, and campaigns connected in one place.

How we work

Practical software shaped around real counters, warehouses, and back offices — with web POS, desktop tills, and clear ledgers your team can trust.

What you get

Stock management, point of sale, bill and account tracking, HR workflows, social campaigns, and multi-branch support — add what you need as you grow.

Why we built it

Less switching. More clarity.

Most teams lose hours copying figures between spreadsheets, tills, and HR sheets. Zeebroo keeps sales, inventory, money, people, and promotions in sync — so you always know what sold today, what is low in stock, and who owes what.

Designed for operators

Flows match how shops and offices actually work, from barcode scan at checkout to paying suppliers on time.

Built to scale with you

Start with stock and POS, then turn on bills, HR, campaigns, and extra branches when you are ready.

Supported by people

Our team helps you set up companies, branches, and staff so everyone starts on the right foot.
